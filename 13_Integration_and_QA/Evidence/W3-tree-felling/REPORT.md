State: committed locally on `deploy/boxd` (on top of W2 6b0c06d / de45251). **Not pushed and not deployed.** Browser runs used emulated Chromium (software GL) on the local build, routed as `https://astrix.local/`. **No real phone was used.** Wilderness scenario only.

## Rules as built (`Game/src/sim/felling.ts`)

**Content v3.** A new wilderness world records `"wildContent":3` in its journal header; its snapshot `wild` block gains a canonical `fell` block (damage records, swing counter, trunks, stockpiles, bounded event list, wilderness woodland). v1 and v2 saves keep their exact bytes, load and replay; the app then sends one journaled `upgrade_wilderness {content:3}` (v1 → v2 → v3 also works). New genesis pin (seed 1337, generator v2): `158e989e4c10cf13`. The v2 genesis `3c3a0c8e4090efcf` is unchanged.

**Hit points.** HP = max(2, round(M013 timber total × hardness)); hardness oak/beech 0.65, birch/cherry 0.6, pine/spruce 0.55; a snag ×0.7.
- Every sapling: 2 HP.
- Oak: young 5, mature 10, old 13.
- Birch mature: 5.
- Pine old: 9.
- Beech old: 12.

**`chop {entityId, at}`.** One journaled command per swing. It uses `at` (player position in dm), the same convention as `pickup`, not separate `x, z` fields. It is applied immediately on submit and validated in this order:
1. an axe is in hand (`no_axe`) and is not broken (`broken_tool`);
2. the tree exists (its chunk is generated by the sim, so replay never depends on what was loaded) and is standing (`not_choppable` for a stump or gap);
3. reach is at most 2.5 m from the trunk surface (`out_of_reach`);
4. rate: at most 4 swings (`chop` and `cut_log` together) per 2 s world tick (`too_fast`). This is deterministic. The client paces swings at 650 ms.

Each accepted swing removes 1 HP and 1 axe durability (the stone axe has 60). Events: `CHOP_STARTED` on the first hit, `TREE_DAMAGED` on every hit.

**Felling (HP 0).**
- Events: `TREE_FELLED` and `TRUNK_CREATED`.
- The entity keeps its id and species and becomes an M013 stump: a lifecycle record in `fell.woodland`, with a coppice roll on the woodland stream and resprout or rot days from `GROWTH`.
- A trunk record `t:<n>` is created, lying in the DIRS16 direction most nearly away from the player.
  - Length = species height × stage × scale (old beech 7.9 m).
  - Radius = trunk width × stage (old beech 0.36 m).
- Crown branches land as a `branch` pile: young 1, mature 2, old 3.
- Nothing enters the bag. One-step `harvest` stays refused.

**Trunk collision.** A trunk thicker than 0.45 m blocks walking below its top, so the player walks around it or jumps over. A thinner one (sapling or young) is stepped over.

**`cut_log {trunkId, at}`.**
- Validation: same axe and rate checks; reach is measured to the nearest point of the remaining trunk.
- A thin trunk (r < 20 cm) needs 1 swing per log, a thick one 2.
- Each log comes off the far end as a `log` pile (qty 1) beside the trunk (`LOGS_CREATED`). The trunk shortens and is removed after its last log.
- Logs per tree = ceil(M013 total / M013 cut), so each log is one M013 cut of wood. Examples: old oak 4, mature oak 3, mature birch 3, sapling 1.

**Logs.**
- A log weighs 9 against the bag's 30: with the axe (3) a player carries at most 3, fewer with other items.
- Picking up a log uses the existing `pickup` (outcome event `LOGS_COLLECTED`).

**Delivery (choice: a minimal stockpile marker, so the event exists before W4).**
- `mark_stockpile {at}` places a marker `s:<n>` where the player stands.
  - It is refused on water, within 6 m of another stockpile, on a trunk, or beyond 4 markers.
  - Event: `STOCKPILE_MARKED`.
- `drop` of logs within 3 m of a marker is a delivery: the logs join one stacked pile at the marker, the marker's `delivered` count rises, and the outcome records `LOGS_DELIVERED` and the `stockpileId`.
- Logs dropped elsewhere make a plain pile.
- W4 storage buildings can take over the marker.

**Event log.** Every event is recorded in the outcome of the command that caused it, and in `fell.events` (the last 40, numbered `seq`, with tick, ref and n). Both are hashed and saved. The app exposes them in `__metrics.wild.felling.events` and in a small HUD feed.

**Regrowth (M013 lifecycle, ported without the homestead gate).** `treeLife` now also reads the wilderness woodland, so v3 wilderness trees age through the M013 stages from the v3 epoch. Each tick, due record events run, at most 16 per tick:
- a coppicing stump resprouts as a sapling (same id, origin `sprout`);
- any other stump rots, and after the species' gap time the spot regrows in place (origin `regrow`). This is a wilderness deviation: the wilderness has no forester or managed seeding window, so the spot regrows the way a fallen snag's spot does in M013.

## Presentation (`render/felling.ts`, `render/pickups.ts`, `app/explore.ts`, `app/wild-game.ts`)
- **Damaged tree.** A damaged tree within 40 m (at most 6 of them) is drawn by a proxy of its own block model and hidden from the nature batch with `NatureView.setHidden`. The proxy has a dark notch band and a pale cut facing the player, both sized from the sim's HP. It shakes for 0.45 s when a swing lands.
- **Fall.** On felling, the proxy rotates about its foot toward the sim's fall direction (ease-in, 1.5 s, starting 0.22 s after the swing). The M013 stump is drawn at once, and the trunk record is drawn when the fall ends.
- **Models.** Trunks (with cut ends), single logs, log stacks and stockpile markers are block-style InstancedMeshes.
- **Swing.** An accepted swing plays a two-handed animation (wind-up, strike, recover in 0.5 s) facing the target. A refusal shows its reason in a toast and makes no swing.
- **Controls.**
  - E or the touch Use button acts. Hold it to keep swinging; holding after a pickup does not start chopping.
  - Click or tap a tree or trunk to target it.
  - The Use button label follows the action: Chop / Cut log / Pick up / Deliver.
  - The Bag panel has "Mark stockpile here".

## Not done / limitations
- Not deployed; no real device. Timings come from a scripted, software-GL run (screenshots take about 1.3 s), so swing counts are exact but wall-clock seconds are approximate.
- `LOGS_DELIVERED` uses a player marker, not a W4 storage building. Nothing consumes logs or wood yet.
- `cut_log` reach is measured to the nearest point of the remaining trunk, so a whole trunk can be cut from beside its base.
- Reach trusts the client-reported position, as in W2.
- v3 wilderness trees now age, die and regrow over in-game days (M013 natural cycle). There is no seeding into new spots or forester in the wilderness. A rotted stump regrows in place.
- A broken axe stays in the bag at 0 durability; there is no repair.
- The log weight is the same for every species and size.
