## What is authoritative

Everything below is schema-3 simulation state. It changes only through journaled commands or the journaled real-time `tick`, it replays exactly, and it is saved with the player's own world.

| Part | State | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Fields | position, size (2–10 m per side), crop, stage, growth, soil moisture, digging progress | **The player marks each field** (F, or Farm → Plan a new field): where it goes, how big it is and what grows there. The fence costs wood from the bag (1 per 4 m of edge). A field must be within 45 m of the farmhouse, on soil, flat (at most 1 m of slope) and dry, and clear of trees, rocks, buildings and other fields. The planner explains every reason a spot is rejected. Up to 12 fields, 400 m² in total. |
| Crops | wheat, potatoes, flax | Each 9 m² unit needs 1 seed. Growth happens only in daylight and only while the soil is wet. A sprouted crop left dry for 45 ticks withers and its seed is lost; seed in dry soil waits until watered. Harvest per unit: wheat 3 food + 2 seed + 2 straw; potatoes 6 food + 1 seed (slower, needs less water); flax 5 straw + 2 seed. |
| Villagers | role, position, energy, hunger, what they carry, current task | Ada (farmer) and Bram (herder) plan real errands that move real resources. The farmer digs, fetches seed from the store, sows, draws water at the well, waters and harvests, then carries the harvest to the store. The herder fills troughs, carries straw from the store, collects eggs, milks and shears. Both carry one load at a time, eat from the store when hungry, and sleep in the farmhouse at night. |
| Animals | 6 species, sex, age, position, hunger, product, pregnancy | Animals graze their pasture, which regrows by area, and eat trough feed when it runs low. They drink trough water. Fed adults produce eggs, milk or wool, and fed adults breed up to each enclosure's capacity. An animal left without food for 240 ticks dies. |
| Buildings | farmhouse, well, coop, sheep and goat pen, pig sty, paddock, barn | Each building has a function in the rules: the farmhouse is where villagers sleep, the well supplies water, enclosures house animals and hold their trough. The barn is extra storage (300) beside the 200-unit store. Models are drawn from the same definitions. |
| Store | the founder stockpile plus the barn | Seed reserved for sowing waiting fields cannot be withdrawn. Milk and eggs partly spoil at dawn. |
| Steward (AI agent) | on/off, per-action policy, open requests | See below. |

The player can do every job themselves with the Use button or E. That covers dig, sow, draw water, water, harvest, feed, fill troughs, collect eggs, milk and shear. It uses the player's own bag and follows the same rules as the villagers.

## Steward (AI agent) governance

The steward runs in the owner's browser as a deterministic planner over authoritative state. It can only issue commands tagged `actor: 'steward'`. The simulation checks each one against the owner's policy:

- **Reassign jobs:** default *may act*.
- **Fence new fields:** default *ask me first*. The steward then files a request in the Farm panel, and the owner approves or declines it.

Every decision is in the journal, with the steward named as the actor. The owner is never blocked by steward policy.

## Compatibility

`homestead` is an optional snapshot field. Worlds without a homestead keep their exact bytes and hashes, and the pinned genesis hash test is unchanged. Existing schema-3 worlds are founded automatically on load by an ordinary journaled `found_homestead` command. Founding clears the trees inside building footprints, and their wood goes into the store. Schema-1 and 2 worlds are untouched.

## Changed alongside

- The object inspector popup is off for players. It is now a developer tool at `?inspect=1`.
- The sky follows settlement time once a homestead exists, so night falls when villagers go to bed.

## Not done yet (honest)

- **Vegetation overhaul:** block-built tree species, flowers and grasses are not in this checkpoint. Trees still use the faceted crowns.
- **Wildlife beyond deer, hares and fish:** no foxes, wolves or birds yet. Wildlife remains presentation-only ambient life.
- **Villager movement:** villagers walk straight lines, with no pathfinding around fences, and do not open gates. Animals stay inside their enclosure rectangles by rule, not by collision.
- **Product uses:** wool, milk and eggs have no uses beyond food (eggs and milk are eaten) until crafting arrives. Pig meat, draught cattle and horse transport are listed as future purposes.
- **Steward scope:** the steward covers two kinds of decision only (jobs and new fields).
